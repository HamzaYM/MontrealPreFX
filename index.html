§ 08 — La listeWhat to bring · août = pretty perfect weather

What to pack.

High of 78°F, low of 60°F, mostly sunny, the occasional dramatic rainstorm. Pack like a city — not a beach.

Essentials

  • Passport — international border
  • Walking shoes you trust
  • A light layer for evenings
  • Compact umbrella
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Sunglasses

Looking-the-part

  • One nice dinner outfit
  • Something for a rooftop
  • Comfortable jeans — Plateau uniform
  • A great jacket — for photos

For specific plans

  • Swimsuit — Bota Bota spa
  • Sandals or flip-flops
  • Running shoes — canal run
  • A blanket / picnic gear (we'll share)

Don't forget

  • Canadian-friendly cell plan
  • A credit card with no FX fee
  • Some CAD cash for markets
  • Power adapter — same as US
  • Patience for the border
